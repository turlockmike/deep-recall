# Home utilities

## Electricity
We compared five fixed-rate plans in March. The provider's customer service was slow but polite.
After a long call we switched to the **Saver Plus 12** plan: 12.76 cents per kWh, a $125 bill credit
at 1,000 kWh or more, and a $150 early-termination fee. The contract runs 12 months.

## Water
The water utility bills every two months. A leak in the irrigation line in 2025 doubled one bill.

## Internet
Fiber, 1 Gbps symmetric. The router lives in the hall closet.
